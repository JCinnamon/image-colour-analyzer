Batch Image Analyzer

Measure colour across a whole set of images. Everything runs locally in your browser.

How it works
  • Local files (recommended) — drag and drop images or select a folder. Nothing is uploaded; analysis happens in your browser. EXIF metadata (GPS, date, camera) is read from JPEGs and from PNGs that carry an eXIf chunk, including cut-outs exported by Image Cutter with GPS/EXIF preserved.
  • Image URLs — direct links to .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif, or .bmp files. The image host must permit cross-origin access (CORS); images that block it will fail with a clear message.
  • PNG transparency is respected — choose whether transparent areas are composited onto white, black, or grey, or excluded entirely so only opaque pixels are analysed.
  • Image-level metrics — mean and median hue, saturation, value, CIELAB L* and C*ab, with spread (std and IQR) and hue circular variance; plus colourfulness, redness index, correlated colour temperature (CCT), RMS contrast, dark-channel haze, warm/cool/neutral proportions, and hue entropy.
  • Spatial structure (optional) — row and column colour profiles, an N×N grid/tile table, per-cluster centroid and spatial spread, and Moran's I spatial autocorrelation of lightness.
  • Outputs — cluster-level, image-level, and optional pixel-level, hue-histogram, and spatial (profiles, grid) tables, each downloadable as CSV with consistent SPACE_CHANNEL_STAT column names (e.g. HSV_V_mean, LAB_L_median, LCH_C_mean). Per-image visualizations (palette, cluster-region, grid, row/column profile, and H/S/V histogram) open from a dropdown on each result and download as PNG.
